The Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration (FMCSA) establishes regulations that are critical for every freight broker and dispatcher to understand. The core component for drivers is the Hours of Service (HOS) rules.

Key HOS Rules to Remember

  • 14-Hour Shift Window: A driver may not drive beyond the 14th consecutive hour after coming on duty.
  • 11-Hour Driving Limit: Within that 14-hour window, a driver is allowed a maximum of 11 hours of actual driving time.
  • 10-Hour Break: Drivers must be off duty for at least 10 consecutive hours before their shift clock resets.
"Understanding the 30-minute break rule is where most dispatchers make mistakes. A driver must take a 30-minute break after 8 cumulative hours of driving."
